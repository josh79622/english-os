# Profile

Stable facts about me, carried into every briefing so the coach can ask
about my actual life and I always have something to answer with. Scenario
facts only cover the scene; most stalls happen on the ordinary questions
around it — what do you do, where do you live, what did you do on the
weekend.

**This file is hand-written and is not derived from anything.** Nothing
regenerates it. Edit it whenever something changes, and keep every line
short enough to say out loud without thinking.

Lines that still say TODO are skipped, so a half-filled profile is fine —
fill it in as you notice which questions catch you out.
